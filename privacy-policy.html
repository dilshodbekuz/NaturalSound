Privacy Policy for Calm Sounds

Last updated: June 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application Calm Sounds (package uz.apprica.naturalsound, the "App") handles information. By using the App you agree to the practices described below.

Information stored on your device

The App stores the following only locally on your device and never transmits it to us or any third party: your chosen display name, language preference, theme preference, and your personal listening statistics (daily seconds, sessions, and streak). This data stays on your device and is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

Information collected through third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect information to operate, improve, and monetize the App:

  • Google AdMob — displays ads and may collect your advertising identifier, IP address (approximate, coarse location), and device information to serve and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Firebase Analytics (Google) — collects anonymous usage and device information to help us understand how the App is used.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — collects crash logs and diagnostic device information so we can fix problems.
  • Firebase Authentication (Google) — creates an anonymous account identifier so the App can load its sound catalog. This identifier is not linked to your real identity.
  • Firebase Realtime Database (Google) — provides the catalog of sounds. The App only reads this data; it does not upload any personal data to it.

These services are provided by Google. Their handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

How we use information

Collected information is used to operate the App, display and measure advertisements, analyze and improve performance, and diagnose crashes. We do not sell your personal data.

Advertising

The App contains advertisements served by Google AdMob. Ads may be personalized based on your advertising identifier. You can reset or limit ad personalization at any time in your device settings under Google > Ads.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored preferences and statistics can be deleted at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. To request deletion of any data held by the third-party services above, contact us at the address below.
